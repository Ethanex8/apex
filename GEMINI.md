# Apex Engineering & Couch-to-Silicon Platform Invariants

## 1. Zero-Throwaway Hardware Convergence
- **One Cohesive Machine**: Every subsystem (`brain`, `drive`, `power`, `limb`, `walker`, `sight`) must function as a verifiable standalone desktop build *and* bolt directly into the final `~2.5 kg` autonomous quadruped without circuit-board redesigns (`respins`) or throwaway subsystems.
- **Locked Interface Contracts**: All electrical rails (`21.9V` max 6-cell `LiFePO4` touch-safe Extra-Low Voltage / `SELV` bus, regulated `5V`/`3.3V` logic rails), communication frames (dual `5 Mbps` noise-immune differential motor bus / `CAN-FD`, `sight <-> brain` framed velocity link, sub-microsecond programmable-chip camera sync pulse), and mechanical mounting patterns are canonically specified in `VISION.md` and `systems/*/README.md`.
- **Home-Safe by Design**: Physical hardware targets `~2.5 kg` total mass, `< 22V` touch-safe lithium-iron-phosphate (`LiFePO4`) power, `< 2.5 N·m` hand-backdrivable joints, current-limited bench power-supply (`PSU`) bring-up before connecting batteries, physical mushroom emergency-stop (`E-stop`) cutoffs, and factory-assembled boards/parts (`turnkey PCBA`, industrial powder-fused nylon `MJF`, and `CNC` milling) to avoid indoor solder-paste or high-temp plastic fumes.

## 2. Textbook-Style Writing for Software Engineers (High Rigor, Zero Unexplained Hardware Jargon)
- **Reader Persona**: Write like a classic systems engineering textbook (*Harris & Harris*, *xv6*, *Hennessy & Patterson*) aimed at an experienced, mathematically strong software/systems engineer who has little prior electrical, circuit-board, or mechanical shop background. Never dumb down equations or physics; never assume familiarity with datasheet acronyms or shop-floor shorthand.
- **Mechanism First, Standard Term Second**: Whenever a hardware, circuit, or mechanical term appears, explain *what it physically is or does* in plain prose first, then introduce the standard industry term/acronym in parentheses—e.g., *"step-down switching voltage regulator (**buck converter**)"*, *"local charge-reservoir capacitors (**MLCC decoupling**)"*, or *"radar-like trace-echo testing (**Time-Domain Reflectometry / TDR**)"*. Always label chip/material part numbers by their physical job (e.g., *"`W9825G6KH-6` 32 MB external dynamic RAM"*) and disambiguate overloaded acronyms (*battery State of Charge* vs. *processor System-on-Chip*).
- **Systems-to-Physics Bridges & Self-Contained Derivations**: Anchor physical phenomena in familiar CS/systems primitives (e.g., decoupling capacitors as a tiny local L1 charge cache masking wire-inductance latency; flip-flop setup/hold windows as distributed latch deadlines; motor frame transforms as coordinate rotations turning AC waves into steady DC numbers), then derive the exact math, physics, or timing right alongside optional deeper textbook pointers (`reference_reading`).
- **Markdown & Math Hygiene**: Reserve `$...$` strictly for multi-term LaTeX equations. Escape literal currency (`\$`) in prose (never inside backticks, where `\` renders literally), write *"about"* or *"roughly"* instead of `~` in prose (since single `~...~` triggers strikethrough in GitHub Flavored Markdown), and wrap identifiers/equations containing `_`, `*`, `<=`, `>=`, or `<->` in backticks or Unicode (`≤`, `≥`, `↔`).
- **Engineering Naming Only**: Use plain-English subsystem IDs (`brain`, `drive`, `power`, `limb`, `walker`, `sight`) and sequential step IDs (`<system>-NN`). Never introduce academic course codes, credit tiers, or opaque capstone numbers.

## 3. Declarative Single Source of Truth (`roadmap.yaml` & `scripts/build.py`)
- **Canonical Roadmap**: `roadmap.yaml` is the single source of truth for all 6 systems, phases, atomic `couch` and `bench` steps, verification commands, first-principles concepts, home-workbench procurement gates, and step completion state (`pending`, `in_review`, `approved`).
- **Automated Compilation & Validation**: Never hand-edit `ROADMAP.md` or `README.md` directly. After modifying `roadmap.yaml` (directly or via `scripts/step.py`), always run `python3 scripts/build.py` to validate all schema, dependency-graph (`DAG`) acyclicity, and step-completeness invariants and regenerate `ROADMAP.md` and `README.md`, then confirm zero drift with `python3 scripts/build.py --check`.

## 4. Couch-to-Silicon Atomic Review Contract
- **Strictly One Step at a Time**: Agents execute at most one atomic step from `roadmap.yaml` per turn (`~50–150 LOC` for software or hardware-description code steps, or one bounded circuit-board/3D-CAD milestone). Never bundle multiple steps together.
- **Verify Before Requesting Review**: Every `couch` step must pass its automated verification suite (`Verilator` simulation, `Yosys`/`nextpnr` FPGA synthesis, KiCad schematic electrical-rule checks `ERC` and layout design-rule checks `DRC`, trace-impedance/power-network solvers, `MuJoCo` physics simulation, or `pytest`) inside the workspace before entering `in_review`.
- **Mobile Review Packet + Hard Approval Gate**: Present each completed step with (1) a 5-minute textbook-style First-Principles Concept Brief, (2) interactive visual diagrams, timing waveforms, or copper layer renders, (3) a guided diff tour backed by a clean GitHub PR/commit, and (4) 2–3 intuition-check questions. Wait for explicit user `LGTM` before marking the step `approved` (`python3 scripts/step.py approve <step-id>`) and advancing to the next step.
