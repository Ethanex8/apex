# Apex Engineering & Couch-to-Silicon Platform Invariants

## 1. Zero-Throwaway Hardware Convergence
- **One Cohesive Machine**: Every subsystem (`brain`, `drive`, `power`, `limb`, `walker`, `sight`) must function as a verifiable standalone desktop build *and* bolt directly into the final `~2.5 kg` autonomous quadruped without circuit-board redesigns (`respins`) or throwaway subsystems.
- **Locked Interface Contracts**: All electrical rails (`21.9V` max 6-cell `LiFePO4` touch-safe Extra-Low Voltage / `SELV` bus, regulated `5V`/`3.3V` logic rails), communication frames (dual `5 Mbps` noise-immune differential motor bus / `CAN-FD`, `sight <-> brain` framed velocity link, sub-microsecond programmable-chip camera sync pulse), and mechanical mounting patterns are canonically specified in `VISION.md` and `systems/*/README.md`.
- **Home-Safe by Design**: Physical hardware targets `~2.5 kg` total mass, `< 22V` touch-safe lithium-iron-phosphate (`LiFePO4`) power, `< 2.5 N·m` hand-backdrivable joints, current-limited bench power-supply (`PSU`) bring-up before connecting batteries, physical mushroom emergency-stop (`E-stop`) cutoffs, and factory-assembled boards/parts (`turnkey PCBA`, industrial powder-fused nylon `MJF`, and `CNC` milling) to avoid indoor solder-paste or high-temp plastic fumes.

## 2. Textbook-Style Writing for General Programmers & Aspiring Engineers (High Rigor, Zero Unexplained Jargon)
- **Reader Persona**:
  - Target an engineer or aspiring engineer who knows **general programming plus undergraduate math and physics**, does not instantly recall every specialist topic, and has **zero prior background** in computer architecture, chip-design toolchains, electrical circuits, or mechanical shop jargon.
  - Never dumb down equations or physics, never assume familiarity with *any* hardware acronym, mnemonic, vendor name, or shorthand, and **never anchor explanations in niche software specialties** (database internals, distributed consensus, kernel paging, managed runtimes)—bridge instead from everyday programming (`if`/`else`, arrays, loops, functions, structs, byte buffers).
- **Mechanism First, Immediate Adjacency (`No Acronym Soup`)**:
  - State **what a new term does in plain everyday language first** and place its **expanded name / acronym immediately adjacent** to its noun phrase—never separated by a parenthetical or em-dash clause, and never stacking 3+ new acronyms in one sentence.
- **General Principles, Abstraction Boundaries & Self-Explanatory Visuals**:
  - Never drop project-specific sizing or clock constants into scale-independent conceptual explanations; ground system constants physically when their subsystem is introduced.
  - Explain higher-layer architectural behavior strictly via its observable contract (operand widths, state transitions)—never leak unintroduced lower-layer ports, pins, or microarchitectural circuit topologies/cycle counts before hardware-implementation chapters.
  - Lead comparative sections with a direct plain-English takeaway, justify implementation choices honestly without unmotivated slogans, and derive non-obvious bit-manipulation or algorithmic formulas **before** transcluding the code snippet that implements them.
  - Place single-concept **SVG-first** visual diagrams (static or interactive `<svg>` depicting geometry, signal/wire routing, plots, or aligned lane schematics—never text-only calculator cards duplicating equations) right where analogies are first introduced, using **one unambiguous title** (never stacking a separate pill/category title beside a main title), aligned bit-slice labels (`Bits hi..lo`), clean input provenance, and exact insertion-line audits for zero forward symbols.
- **Concise Headings, Zero Front-Matter Banners & Semantic Line Breaks**:
  - Keep chapter H1 titles (`<= 6` words) and `##`/`###` headings (`<= 8` words, closing with `## <N>. Problems`) crisp without `&`, `Hook: Subtitle` colons, or parenthetical clutter.
  - Open chapters directly with prose (zero front-matter banners) and open every `##`/`###` section with a calm 2–4 sentence conceptual bridge strictly aligned to its heading.
  - Write Markdown prose as **one sentence per line** (single newlines, never trailing `<br>` breaks) so PR reviewers can comment cleanly on any sentence.
- **Code-Only Backticks, Restrained Boldface & Math Hygiene**:
  - Reserve backticks strictly for literal code, instruction/register tokens, literal constants, CLI flags, and paths—never English acronyms, language/tool proper nouns, prose numbers/units, or raw TeX.
  - Keep boldface (`**...**`) for newly defined terms (`<= 6` words) and bullet lead-ins, reserve `$...$` for math, escape literal currency (`\$`), and write *"about"* or *"roughly"* instead of `~`.
- **Proof Rigor, Lemma Order & Step-by-Step Display Algebra**:
  - Derive every mathematical or bitwise equivalence claim *and* claimed exception in strict dependency order without forward corollary citations.
  - Refresh prerequisite math/physics identities and introduce closely related elementary operators coherently in one concise foundational reference before downstream sections rely on them, define every operator before bounding corner cases, justify representations, transfer shared results via equivalence instead of redundant parallel proofs, and explicitly prove rounding directions.
  - Pair newly proved reusable algebraic theorems immediately with practical usage intuition (what term-wise substitutions hold, which assumptions break when one operand or inverse fails) so readers build fluency before later proofs invoke the rule.
  - Open every proof or helper derivation with 1–2 plain, low-symbol sentences giving intuitive meaning of **what** it shows and **why** it matters right here—never a dense prose-math wall, stiff `"Next we prove..."` announcements, or forward section catalogs—and state any shared bridge **once** before a parallel derivation list rather than repeating boilerplate citations on every item.
  - Place inline `#### Exercise` drills **selectively** only where active practice builds genuinely new, error-prone mathematical or **symbolic algebraic manipulation** fluency (never repeating routine arithmetic for simple operations already illustrated by widgets and `## <N>. Problems`), embedding a compact collapsible terse solution directly beneath **each individual question or sub-part** using strictly previously introduced notation.
  - When proving that one hardware circuit satisfies two interpretations, present the bridge as a justified aligned derivation starting from the target interpretation of the output wires and stepping cleanly through the circuit's primary specification to the desired target expression—and explicitly call out the architectural breakthrough whenever pure algebra eliminates duplicate hardware, mode bits, or extra logic.
  - Never describe sub-terms, indices, or exponents of an equation before that equation appears, and motivate every algebraic precondition by stating which step or theorem requirement depends on it.
  - Pair step-by-step clarity with **proof economy**: choose the shortest, most transparent valid proof over multi-block symbolic ceremony or auxiliary-variable detours, never cram multi-step numeric or symbolic worked examples into dense prose paragraphs (promote them to compact aligned LaTeX blocks, tables, or SVGs), keep motivating sentences single-purpose, match intro prose to displayed symbols, and check every causal connective (*because*, *consequently*, *therefore*, *thus*) by strict entailment.
- **Logical Macro/Micro Ordering & Base-Before-Extension Layering**:
  - Establish intrinsic properties of each representation or model (range, uniqueness, wrap behavior) where it is introduced, co-locate specialized helpers with their motivating subsystem, and complete base architectures before custom extensions.
  - Scope every theory and subsystem section strictly to concepts needed immediately; co-locate operation- or instruction-motivated mechanics with the downstream section that first introduces and consumes them.
  - Order sequential items from simplest/most fundamental direct properties to derived implementation techniques.
  - Start a fresh paragraph when transitioning from intuition and concrete examples into a formal theorem or derivation.
  - Never braid two separate mechanisms or theorems inside one paragraph or numbered item—introduce each operation with plain-English intuition and its own motivating example before formal edge cases or equations.
- **Tables & Lists Over Enumerative Prose, Balanced End-to-End Rigor**:
  - Present 3+ parallel items sharing structured attributes in scannable Markdown tables or structured lists rather than multi-clause prose walls, balancing lead-in prose proportionally across every category the table introduces.
  - Distribute mathematical and quantitative engineering derivations evenly across *all* sections without early pedantry or late datasheet flattening.
- **Directness, Zero Meta-Commentary & Engineering Naming**:
  - Introduce every subsystem, module, and mechanism directly by what it **is** and **does**—never open with meta-commentary or negative foils—and use plain-English subsystem IDs (`brain`, `drive`, `power`, `limb`, `walker`, `sight`) and sequential step IDs (`<system>-NN`).
