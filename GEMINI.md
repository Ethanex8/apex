# Apex Engineering & Couch-to-Silicon Platform Invariants

## 1. Zero-Throwaway Hardware Convergence
- **One Cohesive Machine**: Every subsystem (`brain`, `drive`, `power`, `limb`, `walker`, `sight`) must run as a verifiable standalone desktop build *and* bolt directly into the `about 2.5 kg` autonomous quadruped without circuit-board redesigns (`respins`) or throwaway subsystems.
- **Locked Interface Contracts**: Electrical rails (`21.9V` max 6-cell `LiFePO4` `SELV`, `5V`/`3.3V` logic), communication frames (dual `5 Mbps` `CAN-FD`, `sight <-> brain` link, camera sync pulse), and mounting patterns are canonically specified in `VISION.md` and `systems/*/README.md`.
- **Home-Safe by Design**: Target `about 2.5 kg` total mass, `< 22V` touch-safe `LiFePO4` power, `< 2.5 N·m` backdrivable joints, current-limited bench `PSU` bring-up, mushroom `E-stop` cutoffs, and factory-assembled `PCBA`/`MJF`/`CNC` parts.

## 2. Textbook-Style Writing & Single-Source Rules
Detailed audit checklists live strictly in `.agents/skills/technical-writing-author/references/rubric_*.md`. Every chapter follows **The Apex Exposition Standard**, anchored on one guiding philosophy supported by every rule below:
- **Guiding Philosophy (Rule of Four)**: Treat **Words, Visuals, Numbers, and Algebra** as four equal representations, each with pros and cons—pick whichever mode(s) deliver maximum clarity with minimum complexity, and pair them so each covers another's blind spots.
- **Universal First-Principles Reader**: Write for anyone with **general programming (`if`/`else`, arrays, loops, functions, structs, byte buffers) and foundational STEM literacy** (algebra, intro calculus/linear algebra/physics)—assuming zero prior hardware/robotics specialization, zero niche software internals (databases, consensus, kernels, runtimes), and zero instant theorem recall.
- **Feynman Mechanics & Just-in-Time Refreshers**: Explain *what physically or algorithmically happens*—and why the naive model breaks—before naming jargon (`expanded name / acronym` immediately adjacent, never after an em-dash or 3+ stacked acronyms); refresh foundational math/physics with brief 1–2 sentence intuitive anchors right before use.
- **Concreteness Fading & Zero Logic Leaps**: Bridge smoothly across concrete numbers, spatial `<svg>` visuals (one title, `Bits hi..lo`, zero premature symbols), and concise abstraction; reserve multi-step proofs strictly for genuinely non-obvious invariants while stating every domain, discreteness condition, and boundary explicitly so zero leaps occur.
- **Cognitive Load Hygiene & Woven Examples**: Keep concrete examples woven naturally inside each concept's flow—never detached `#### Example` headings, exercises, problem sets, or `<details>` toggles—tabulate `3+` structured items cleanly, and cut proof theater or cross-format repetition.
- **Clean Layering & Abstraction Boundaries**: Order stateless/simple primitives ahead of coupled multi-part mechanisms, describe higher layers strictly via observable contracts without leaking unintroduced internals, complete base standards before custom extensions, co-locate helpers with consumers, and keep project constants out of general theory.
- **Directness, Typography & Naming**: Open chapters and sections directly (zero banners or negative foils), keep H1 (`<= 6` words) and `##`/`###` (`<= 8` words, no `---` above `## `) crisp, write one sentence per line, use code-only backticks, boldface `<= 6` words, and canonical subsystem/`<system>-NN` IDs.

## 3. Declarative Single Source of Truth (`roadmap.yaml` & `scripts/build.py`)
- **Canonical Roadmap & Textbook Outline**: `roadmap.yaml` is the single source of truth for all 6 systems, phases, 186 `couch`/`bench` steps, verification commands, step statuses (`pending`, `in_review`, `approved`), and the 8-part, 34-chapter syllabus (`book.parts`).
- **Automated Compilation & Validation**: Never hand-edit `ROADMAP.md`, `README.md`, or `docs/CHAPTER_INDEX.md`; after editing `roadmap.yaml` or chapters, run `python3 scripts/build.py` and confirm zero drift with `python3 scripts/build.py --check`.

## 4. Couch-to-Silicon Atomic Review Contract & Iterative Open-PR Loop
- **Strictly One Step per PR**: Execute at most one atomic step from `roadmap.yaml` per cycle (`about 50–150 LOC` or one bounded hardware/CAD milestone); never bundle multiple steps together.
- **Code & Hardware Quality (`code-author`)**: Every `.py`, `.sh`, `.sv`, `.c`, `.h`, `.S`, and `.vue` simulation artifact passes frozen-prompt sharded Pass-3 audits (`build_review_plan.py --code`), `check_code.py` (AST, Ruff, `mypy`, ShellCheck, C/SV gates), and `>= 95%` test coverage.
- **Multi-Step Chapters (`technical-writing-author`)**: Grow `systems/<system>/chapters/<NN>_<slug>.md` `M:1` across steps, converging full-chapter frozen-prompt sharded audits (`build_review_plan.py --chapter`) plus `sync_book.py --check`, `check_book.py`, `build.py --check`, `BOOK_BASE=/apex/ npm --prefix book run build`, and `check_visuals.py` (light + dark PNGs).
- **Protected `main`, Previews & 1-Tap Merge**: Open one Ready-for-Review `step/<step-id>` PR with `step.py approve <step-id> --pr <URL>` pre-staged, keep the PR open without force-pushing, and review on `/pr-<N>/` until 1-tap Merge deploys `/apex/` and cleans `/pr-<N>/`.
- **PR Feedback Watcher & 1-Hour Idle Pause**: After CI and previews pass, launch `watch_pr_feedback.py --pr <N>` (`IsDaemon=false`, default 1-hour `--max-duration`); if a shorter command timeout hits with no activity, re-arm until 1 hour total idle time, then pause and notify the user.
- **3-Tier Self-Healing Loop (`pr-review-workflow` & `docs/REVIEW_PROCESS.md`)**: Harvest open threads via `harvest_feedback.py`; fast-track Tier 0/1 fixes, or run Gate 1 -> Step 2 recall/precision -> Step 3 convergence -> `docs/calibration/<skill>.md` -> Step 4 replies -> background `watch_pr_feedback.py --pr <N>`.
