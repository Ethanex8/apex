# Apex Engineering & Couch-to-Silicon Platform Invariants

## 1. Zero-Throwaway Hardware Convergence
- **One Cohesive Machine**: Every subsystem (`brain`, `drive`, `power`, `limb`, `walker`, `sight`) must run as a verifiable standalone desktop build *and* bolt directly into the `about 2.5 kg` autonomous quadruped without circuit-board redesigns (`respins`) or throwaway subsystems.
- **Locked Interface Contracts**: Electrical rails (`21.9V` max 6-cell `LiFePO4` `SELV`, `5V`/`3.3V` logic), communication frames (dual `5 Mbps` `CAN-FD`, `sight <-> brain` link, camera sync pulse), and mounting patterns are canonically specified in `VISION.md` and `systems/*/README.md`.
- **Home-Safe by Design**: Target `about 2.5 kg` total mass, `< 22V` touch-safe `LiFePO4` power, `< 2.5 N·m` backdrivable joints, current-limited bench `PSU` bring-up, mushroom `E-stop` cutoffs, and factory-assembled `PCBA`/`MJF`/`CNC` parts.

## 2. Textbook-Style Writing & Single-Source Rules
Detailed audit checklists live strictly in `.agents/skills/technical-writing-author/references/rubric_*.md`. Every chapter follows these core invariants:
- **Target Reader**: Write for an engineer or aspiring engineer knowing **general programming (`if`/`else`, arrays, loops, functions, structs, byte buffers) plus undergraduate math and physics**, with zero hardware/architecture background—never bridge via database, distributed-consensus, kernel, or runtime internals.
- **Mechanism First & Code-Only Backticks**: State what each new term does in plain language with its **expanded name / acronym** immediately adjacent (never after an em-dash or 3+ stacked acronyms); reserve backticks strictly for literal code, tokens, constants, flags, and paths, keeping boldface `<= 6` words.
- **Abstraction & Layering Discipline**: Describe higher layers strictly via observable contracts without leaking unintroduced ports, pins, circuit topologies, or cycle counts; complete base architectures before custom extensions, co-locate helpers with consumers, and keep project constants out of general theory.
- **Stateless-First Ordering & Defined Mechanics**: Order simpler carry-free primitives ahead of coupled multi-bit mechanisms, and define plus visually illustrate bit-level mechanics before invoking wrap-around abstractions.
- **Concise Headings & Semantic Line Breaks**: Open chapters with direct prose (zero metadata banners), keep H1 (`<= 6` words) and `##`/`###` headings (`<= 8` words, no redundant `---` above `## `) clean, open sections with calm 2–4 sentence bridges, and write **one sentence per Markdown line**.
- **Proof Rigor, Refreshers & Economy**: Refresh prerequisite identities with concrete examples, prove every equivalence/exception/rounding direction in strict dependency order, open proofs with 1–2 plain low-symbol sentences, and choose the shortest transparent proof over multi-block ceremony, redundant symmetric-operator copies, or dense prose-math walls.
- **Dual-Interpretation & Inline Examples**: Present shared-hardware proofs as justified aligned `target(output)` derivations spotlighting eliminated hardware; place visible inline `#### Example` worked examples selectively for non-trivial algebra, boundary traps, or counterexamples—never quizzes, exercises, problem sets, or `<details>` toggles.
- **SVG-First Visuals & Tables**: Place single-concept static or interactive `<svg>` diagrams (never text-only calculator cards, using one unambiguous title, `Bits hi..lo` labels, and exact insertion-line audits) before formalism, and present `3+` structured items in balanced Markdown tables.
- **Directness & Naming**: Open every section, concept, and subsystem directly by what it is and does—never with meta-commentary or negative foils—using `brain`, `drive`, `power`, `limb`, `walker`, `sight` and sequential `<system>-NN` step IDs.

## 3. Declarative Single Source of Truth (`roadmap.yaml` & `scripts/build.py`)
- **Canonical Roadmap & Textbook Outline**: `roadmap.yaml` is the single source of truth for all 6 systems, phases, 186 `couch`/`bench` steps, verification commands, step statuses (`pending`, `in_review`, `approved`), and the 8-part, 34-chapter syllabus (`book.parts`).
- **Automated Compilation & Validation**: Never hand-edit `ROADMAP.md`, `README.md`, or `docs/CHAPTER_INDEX.md`; after editing `roadmap.yaml` or chapters, run `python3 scripts/build.py` and confirm zero drift with `python3 scripts/build.py --check`.

## 4. Couch-to-Silicon Atomic Review Contract & Iterative Open-PR Loop
- **Strictly One Step per PR**: Execute at most one atomic step from `roadmap.yaml` per cycle (`about 50–150 LOC` or one bounded hardware/CAD milestone); never bundle multiple steps together.
- **Code & Hardware Quality (`code-author`)**: Every `.py`, `.sh`, `.sv`, `.c`, `.h`, `.S`, and `.vue` simulation artifact passes frozen-prompt sharded Pass-3 audits (`build_review_plan.py --code`), `check_code.py` (AST, Ruff, `mypy`, ShellCheck, C/SV gates), and `>= 95%` test coverage.
- **Multi-Step Chapters (`technical-writing-author`)**: Grow `systems/<system>/chapters/<NN>_<slug>.md` `M:1` across steps, converging full-chapter frozen-prompt sharded audits (`build_review_plan.py --chapter`) plus `sync_book.py --check`, `check_book.py`, `build.py --check`, `BOOK_BASE=/apex/ npm --prefix book run build`, and `check_visuals.py` (light + dark PNGs).
- **Protected `main`, Previews & 1-Tap Merge**: Open one Ready-for-Review `step/<step-id>` PR with `step.py approve <step-id> --pr <URL>` pre-staged, keep the PR open without force-pushing, and run `watch_pr_feedback.py --pr <N>` asynchronously (`IsDaemon=false`) whenever waiting for human review or 1-tap Merge (deploying root `/apex/` and cleaning `/pr-<N>/`).
- **3-Tier Self-Healing Loop (`pr-review-workflow` & `docs/REVIEW_PROCESS.md`)**: Harvest open threads via `harvest_feedback.py`; fast-track Tier 0/1 fixes, or run Gate 1 -> Step 2 recall/precision -> Step 3 convergence -> `docs/calibration/<skill>.md` -> Step 4 replies -> background `watch_pr_feedback.py --pr <N>`.
